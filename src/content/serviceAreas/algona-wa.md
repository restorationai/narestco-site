## Restoration emergencies common in Algona

Algona sits low in the Green River valley in King County, and that geography shapes what goes wrong in local homes. Heavy fall and winter rain saturates yards and crawlspaces faster than it drains, and homes near the valley floor deal with groundwater pressure against foundations that older subdivisions weren't always built to handle. Winter freeze-thaw cycles crack exposed supply lines and split fittings on older galvanized plumbing, and a burst line in a crawlspace can sit unnoticed for days before it surfaces as a musty smell or a soft spot in the flooring. Wildfire smoke drifting in during late summer is a newer seasonal issue, leaving a fine residue on HVAC systems and soft surfaces even in homes nowhere near a fire line.
