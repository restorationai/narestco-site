## Restoration emergencies common in Dash Point

Dash Point sits directly on Puget Sound, and that proximity shapes the kind of calls we get. Winter storms off the Sound bring wind-driven rain that finds its way under siding and around older window flashing, especially on homes built before modern weather-resistive barrier standards. Bluff-adjacent properties deal with slope drainage and groundwater pressure against foundations that wasn't anticipated when they were built. Combine that with the Pacific Northwest's long wet season, and a leak that sits unnoticed for even a few days can turn into saturated subfloor and the start of mold colonization well before a homeowner notices a smell or a soft spot underfoot.

## Services we provide in Dash Point

Water damage restoration here often starts with a slow, hidden source, a roof flashing failure during a Sound storm, a supply line behind a kitchen wall, or groundwater pushing into a crawlspace after heavy rain. We extract standing water, monitor moisture in framing and subfloor with meters rather than guesswork, and document the drying process for your carrier.

Fire and smoke damage cleanup in Dash Point frequently involves homes with cedar siding and older wood-frame construction, where smoke odor penetrates porous materials fast. We handle structural cleaning, contents, and odor neutralization, not just surface wipe-down.

Storm damage and emergency board-up or tarping cover the wind and debris side of Sound-facing properties, getting a structure weather-tight right after a storm so secondary damage doesn't compound the original loss.
