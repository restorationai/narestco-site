## Restoration emergencies common in Des Moines

Des Moines sits directly on Puget Sound, and that proximity shapes the kind of damage we see. Marine air keeps humidity high year-round, which slows drying times in enclosed crawlspaces and makes mold colonization faster once a leak goes unnoticed. Winter windstorms off the Sound bring down tree limbs and strip shingles on homes that back up to bluffs and wooded lots, and heavy seasonal rain finds its way into older roof assemblies and basement walls that were never built for sustained saturation. Burst or corroded supply lines in homes from the mid-20th century are a recurring call, especially where galvanized pipe is still in service.

Reconstruction closes out the loss, rebuilding drywall, flooring, and framing once drying and remediation are complete, coordinated so the structural repair matches what the insurance scope approved.
