## Restoration emergencies common in Edgewood

Edgewood sits in the Puyallup River valley area of Pierce County, where wet Pacific Northwest winters put sustained pressure on older roofs, gutters, and foundation drainage. Heavy, prolonged rain events (rather than short violent storms) are the dominant pattern here, and that means slow, progressive water intrusion is more common than sudden flooding: a clogged gutter or a failing roof valve can feed moisture into an attic or wall cavity for weeks before a homeowner notices staining or a musty smell. Many homes in the area sit on wooded or sloped lots, so surface water runoff and saturated soil around foundations are a recurring driver of crawlspace moisture and the mold growth that follows it. Wind events and heavy wet snow also bring down tree limbs on roofs and siding, which is the other call we see often once a storm has passed.

## Services we provide in Edgewood
