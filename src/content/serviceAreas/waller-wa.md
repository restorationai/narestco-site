## Restoration emergencies common in Waller

Waller sits in the wet, mild climate zone that defines Western Washington: long stretches of steady winter rain, saturated ground from late fall through early spring, and the occasional windstorm strong enough to take down mature conifers onto roofs and power lines. Homes here deal less with sudden flash flooding and more with slow, persistent moisture intrusion, roof leaks that go unnoticed for weeks, crawlspace dampness that creeps into subfloor insulation, and gutter overflow that backs water against foundations. That slow-moisture pattern is exactly what lets mold get a foothold, since Pacific Northwest humidity gives spores the conditions they need within days of a leak going undetected rather than weeks.
