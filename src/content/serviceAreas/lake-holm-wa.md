## Restoration emergencies common in Lake Holm

Lake Holm's identity as a lake community shapes the kind of damage we see. Homes close to the shoreline deal with higher ambient moisture year-round, which means framing and subfloor materials are already working with less of a safety margin before a plumbing failure or roof leak pushes them into real trouble. Western Washington's wet season adds sustained rainfall and saturated ground for months at a time, so a clogged gutter or a cracked foundation parging doesn't just cause a puddle, it can keep feeding moisture into crawlspaces and sill plates long after the rain stops. Properties on septic systems or private wells, common in this part of unincorporated Pierce County, also see a distinct category of loss: a failed septic line or a sewage backup carries contamination risk that a straightforward water leak doesn't, and it changes how a cleanup has to be scoped and documented for insurance.
