## What should happen on the first call, before a crew ever drives out?

A competent 24-hour restoration company asks specific questions on the first call: the address, the source of the water or the extent of fire damage, whether the water is still flowing, and whether anyone has reached out to your insurance carrier yet. That intake call is where the job starts getting documented correctly.

Have this ready when you call: your address, a rough sense of which rooms are affected, whether the water source has been shut off, and your insurance company's name if you have it handy. Photos taken on your phone before anyone arrives are useful too; they establish a timestamp for your claim.

What you shouldn't expect is a company promising an exact arrival time in minutes over the phone. Response depends on where the crew currently is relative to SR-99 traffic and how many active calls are in queue that night. A company that's honest about dispatching promptly rather than guaranteeing a specific minute count is giving you a more trustworthy answer than one that doesn't.
