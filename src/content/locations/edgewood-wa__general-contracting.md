**Planning a renovation, remodel, or post-damage rebuild in Edgewood?** This small Pierce County city sits along SR 161 between Puyallup and Sumner, and its mix of large-lot rural parcels and newer subdivision construction means no two remodel jobs here start from the same baseline. National Restoration Construction handles kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole-home renovations, and full rebuilds after fire or water loss, working from our Federal Way office just down the corridor.

## Why Edgewood Renovations Look Different

Edgewood incorporated in 1996, but much of the housing stock predates that, built when the area was still unincorporated farmland and timber lots. That means a lot of properties here carry older septic systems, private wells, or well-and-septic combinations instead of city utility hookups, which changes how a kitchen or bathroom remodel gets scoped. Moving a sink or adding a second bath isn't just a plumbing question, it's a question of what the existing septic design can handle. Newer construction closer to SR 161 and the Puyallup border tends to be on more conventional utility service, but we never assume either way until we've pulled the site history.

## Our Process for Remodels and Rebuilds in Edgewood

We start with a walkthrough that looks past the finish work to what's underneath it: panel capacity for a kitchen remodel with new appliances, subfloor condition in older bathrooms, and whether a post-damage rebuild needs to match existing materials for an insurance claim or gives you room to upgrade. From there we scope permitting with Pierce County, since Edgewood contracts its building and permitting functions out rather than running its own department, which can add a step most homeowners don't expect compared to cities with in-house permit counters. Demolition and rough-in follow, then inspections, then finish work. On insurance-driven rebuilds, we document every stage with photos tied to the adjuster's scope so there's no gap between what was damaged and what was rebuilt.

## Permitting and Pierce County Coordination

Because Edgewood routes building permits through Pierce County, timelines for structural changes, additions, or anything touching load-bearing walls run on the county's review schedule rather than a city office you can walk into. We factor that into every remodel quote so you're not blindsided by a permit sitting in review longer than the framing crew expected. For rebuilds tied to an insurance claim, we also coordinate directly with the carrier's scope of loss so the permitted work and the paid-for work line up, which matters when a final inspection has to match both the county's requirements and the claim file.

## Local note

A good number of Edgewood properties sit on septic systems installed decades ago, sized for the household and fixture count at the time. When a remodel adds a bathroom, a larger kitchen island with a second sink, or converts a garage to living space, we check septic capacity and drain field location before finalizing layout, because a design that looks fine on paper can overload a 1980s-era system installed for a three-bedroom house. It's a step that's easy to skip if you're not used to working in Pierce County's unincorporated-turned-incorporated housing stock.
