A fresh remodel in Milton usually looks finished the day the crew packs up, but drywall dust settles into supply registers, paint overspray dulls window glass, and construction grit works into new flooring seams for weeks afterward. Post-construction and specialty cleaning closes that gap between "built" and "livable," and in a city where new residential construction sits right alongside older homes along the Hylebos Creek corridor, the cleanup needs differ depending on what's actually behind the walls.

## Why Milton Properties See Post-Construction Cleaning Issues

Milton straddles the King and Pierce county line between Federal Way and Fife, and the building stock reflects that mixed history: newer subdivisions with open floor plans and ducted HVAC sit near older, smaller homes from the city's rural-era growth. Newer builds generate fine gypsum dust that gets pulled straight into the return air system during framing and drywall, so it recirculates through every room unless the ducts get cleaned before move-in. Older homes being remodeled often have plaster or lath hiding behind newer drywall layers, which breaks down into a gritty dust that's heavier than drywall dust and settles fast, meaning it needs to be wet-wiped rather than just vacuumed off trim and sills.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Milton

We start with a dry pass, pulling out boxes, debris, and loose packaging the crew left behind, before any wet cleaning begins so we're not turning dust into paste. From there we work top to bottom: light fixtures and ceiling fans first, then window tracks and sills where caulk haze and paint overspray collect, then cabinetry faces and interior shelving, then floors last. Baseboards and HVAC vent covers get a dedicated pass because that's where fine dust accumulates even after a general clean looks complete. For additions or whole-home remodels, we also wipe down interior door hardware and light switches, which get handled by multiple trades during construction and often carry more residue than homeowners expect.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

Standard vacuums push fine construction dust back into the air instead of capturing it, so we use HEPA-filtered equipment for drywall and plaster dust, especially in homes built before the 1980s where lead-safe work practices apply to any disturbed painted surfaces. Microfiber systems and pH-appropriate cleaners handle tile, hardwood, and engineered flooring without leaving the hazing that all-purpose cleaners can leave on new finishes. For HVAC runs that carried dust during construction, we clean accessible ductwork and vent boots rather than just wiping the visible grille, since dust trapped further back will shake loose into the home over the following weeks.

## Local note

Because Milton sits across two county lines, permitting and final inspection sign-off can fall under either King or Pierce County depending on the exact address, and occupancy approval sometimes lags behind the contractor's own completion date. We confirm which jurisdiction a project falls under before locking in the final clean date, so the crew isn't finishing a deep clean a week before the inspector actually clears the home for move-in, and the work doesn't need a repeat pass to clear dust that settled during the wait.
