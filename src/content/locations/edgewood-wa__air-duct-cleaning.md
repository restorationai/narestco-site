Edgewood sits in the gap between Puyallup's river valley and the ridge toward Sumner, which means forced-air systems here work overtime pulling in damp, pollen-heavy air for a good chunk of the year and dry wood-stove smoke the rest of it. If your vents are kicking out a musty smell when the furnace kicks on, or the dryer is taking two cycles to do what it used to do in one, that's usually ductwork telling you it's overdue for service, not a sign your HVAC unit is failing.

## Why Edgewood Homes Build Up Duct Debris Faster

A lot of Edgewood's housing stock sits on larger lots with crawl space foundations rather than slab, which is common across this part of Pierce County. Crawl spaces run cooler and damper than conditioned living space, and the supply and return ducts that pass through them pick up condensation on the outside of the metal even when the air inside the duct is clean. Add in homes that still rely on wood stoves or fireplace inserts for supplemental heat during the wetter months, and you get ash and fine particulate pulled into return vents that a slab-on-grade home in a drier climate wouldn't deal with. Pollen from the surrounding farmland and timber also settles into duct runs every spring, which is why a lot of our Edgewood calls come in right after allergy season starts acting up indoors.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process Here

We start with a camera inspection of the supply and return runs so you can see what's actually in there before any work starts, rather than taking our word for it. From there we use negative-pressure HEPA-filtered equipment to pull debris out of the trunk line and branch ducts, following ANSI/ACCA guidance for duct system cleanliness rather than just wiping down what's reachable from the register. For homes with visible mold or heavy mildew smell coming from a crawl-space duct run, we sanitize the interior surfaces with an EPA-registered product after the physical debris is removed, since sanitizing over dirty ductwork just traps contaminants instead of solving the problem. Return grilles, supply boots, and the air handler cabinet itself get cleaned as part of the same visit, not billed as an upsell afterward.

## Dryer Vent Cleaning Matters More on Larger Edgewood Lots

A lot of properties out here have longer dryer vent runs than you'd find in a tighter in-city lot, sometimes routed through an attic or along an exterior wall to reach the nearest outside wall. Longer runs mean more bends, more lint traps along the way, and a higher fire-risk profile if the vent hasn't been cleaned in a few years. We check static airflow at the exterior vent hood as part of the job, since a weak airflow reading at the cap is usually the first sign of a restriction further back in the line, even if the lint trap itself looks clean.

## Local note

Homes built or expanded in Edgewood before gas lines extended further into this part of Pierce County often still run older oil or electric forced-air furnaces tied into ductwork that was never resized when the system was replaced. That mismatch between an updated furnace and original ducting can create pressure imbalances that pull more crawl-space air into the return side than the system was designed for, which shows up as faster dust accumulation even in a relatively new home.
