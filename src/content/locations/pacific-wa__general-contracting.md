Pacific sits on the valley floor where King and Pierce counties meet, and the housing stock reflects decades of small additions, garage conversions, and DIY updates layered onto original mid-century framing. When a kitchen remodel, bathroom overhaul, or full post-damage rebuild is on the table here, the work usually starts with figuring out what's actually behind the walls before anyone talks finishes or fixtures.

## Why Renovation Projects in Pacific Need Local Know-How

Much of Pacific was built out in waves between the 1950s and 1980s, which means a remodel crew is as likely to find galvanized plumbing and ungrounded wiring as modern PEX and romex, sometimes in the same house. The valley's clay-heavy soil also shifts with seasonal moisture, so foundations and slab floors here settle unevenly over time, and a bathroom remodel that looks straightforward on paper can turn up a subfloor that's been slowly sagging toward a low corner for twenty years. Because Pacific straddles two counties, permitting paperwork depends on exactly where a property line falls, which is the first thing we confirm before pulling drawings together.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process

We start with a walkthrough that separates cosmetic wants from structural needs, because in a lot of Pacific homes those two things are tangled together. For a kitchen remodel, that means checking cabinet bases and flooring for old water staining before we price new counters. For a bathroom remodel, it means pulling the trim around the tub or shower to see what the last thirty years of moisture have done to the framing. Once scope is set, we handle design-build drawings, permit submittal, demo, rough-in trades, and finish carpentry under one contract, so a homeowner isn't coordinating five separate subs. On post-damage rebuilds, after fire or water mitigation has already cleared a space, we pick up from the dried-in shell and carry the job through framing, drywall, and final finishes to match the home's existing layout or improve on it.

## Pacific Permitting & Insurance Coordination

Because Pacific sits near the King/Pierce county line, which jurisdiction issues the building permit depends on the parcel's exact location, and we confirm that before submitting plans rather than assuming. For insurance-driven rebuilds, we document pre-existing conditions versus damage-related scope early, since adjusters want a clear line between what the loss caused and what was already there, especially in older homes with prior unpermitted work. We carry a General Contractor Certificate of Registration through the WA State Department of Labor & Industries and are a Lead-Safe Certified Firm, which matters directly in Pacific's older housing stock whenever a remodel disturbs painted surfaces built before 1978.

## Local Note

A good number of Pacific's older homes were built with crawlspaces rather than full basements, and the valley's seasonal water table means those crawlspaces often run damp even when there's no active leak. On remodel jobs that touch plumbing or flooring, we check crawlspace vapor barriers and vent screens before closing anything up, because a remodel built on top of a damp crawlspace tends to surface mold or soft subfloor complaints within a year or two, and it's far cheaper to address during the renovation than to reopen finished work later.
