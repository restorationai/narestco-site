## Why Everett Properties See Lead Paint Issues

A large share of Everett's housing predates 1978, particularly in Bayside and the blocks closer to Riverside where homes were built to serve the mill and waterfront workforce. Layered paint on window sashes, exterior trim, and porch railings in these neighborhoods often includes multiple lead-based coats applied before the ban, sometimes painted over again in the decades since. Moisture from Puget Sound's wet winters accelerates peeling and chalking on exterior siding and sills, which releases lead dust into soil and gutters even without any renovation happening at all. Newer Silver Lake and Pinehurst-Beverly Park subdivisions generally postdate the ban, but older outbuildings, garages, or additions on those same lots can still carry risk if they were moved or built from salvaged materials.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Everett

Work starts with testing, not assumptions. We sample painted surfaces, confirm lead content, and document the scope before anything is disturbed. Containment follows EPA RRP work practices: plastic sheeting sealed to floors and HVAC returns, wet-sanding or wet-scraping instead of dry sanding, and HEPA vacuum cleanup rather than a shop vac or broom. Windows and trim get special attention in Everett's older homes because original wood sashes were repainted repeatedly over the decades, and dry removal of that paint is exactly how lead dust ends up in a crawl space or a window well. Once abatement is complete, we clear the area with a verified cleaning process before dropping containment, and we keep the documentation a lender, buyer, or county inspector may later request.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Lead Paint

HEPA-filtered vacuums and negative air machines are standard on every job, sized to the containment area rather than borrowed from a general cleanup kit. Encapsulation is an option on sound, intact paint where full removal isn't warranted; abatement with wet methods is the standard where paint is failing or where a renovation requires full removal of the substrate. All waste is bagged, labeled, and disposed of according to lead waste handling rules, not mixed into general construction debris.
