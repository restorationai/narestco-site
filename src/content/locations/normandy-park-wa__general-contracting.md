## Why Normandy Park Properties Need Careful Renovation Planning

Many homes here were built in the 1950s and 60s to take advantage of the bluff views over the Sound, which means original electrical panels, galvanized or early copper plumbing, and single-pane window openings are still common behind updated finishes. A bathroom remodel that looks straightforward on paper often uncovers wiring or framing that needs to be brought up to current code before the new tile goes in. Lots graded toward the water also mean retaining walls, drainage, and slope stability are part of almost any addition or exterior renovation conversation, not an afterthought.
