## Why Kent Properties See Duct Issues

Kent sits in a river valley with Green River flood history, and homes near the lower elevations around 98032 and 98030 have a higher incidence of crawlspace moisture finding its way into duct runs that pass beneath the house. Moist ductwork doesn't just smell: it gives dust and debris something to cling to, and over a few seasons that buildup becomes a surface mold can colonize. On East Hill and around Panther Lake, newer construction tends to have tighter duct seals, but we still find construction debris, drywall dust, and sawdust left behind from builds and remodels that never got flushed out of the system before move-in.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Kent

We start with a camera inspection of the supply and return lines so you can see the actual condition before any work begins, not just take our word for it. From there we use negative-pressure HEPA vacuum equipment connected at the main trunk line, then agitate each branch run with rotary brushes or air whips to dislodge debris without damaging flex duct or older sheet metal joints common in Kent Valley's older housing stock. Dryer vent cleaning is handled separately with its own brush and vacuum pass, since lint buildup is a fire hazard that standard duct equipment isn't built to clear safely. On homes near Lake Meridian and West Hill where crawlspace ductwork runs are long, we budget extra time for access and for sealing any disconnected joints we find along the way, since a loose joint undoes the benefit of the cleaning almost immediately.
