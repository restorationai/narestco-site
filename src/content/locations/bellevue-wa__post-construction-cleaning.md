**Post-construction cleanup in Bellevue looks different depending on which end of the city you're standing in.** A final clean on a Crossroads townhome with fresh drywall dust settled into HVAC returns is a different job than detailing a Downtown Bellevue high-rise unit where the builder's film is still on floor-to-ceiling glass. National Restoration Construction handles both: construction debris removal, fine-dust extraction, and the detail-level specialty cleaning that gets a space ready for occupancy or a punch-list sign-off.

## Why Bellevue Properties See Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Bellevue's building mix drives most of the cleanup complexity. Newer multifamily and townhome construction in Factoria and Crossroads generates heavy gypsum dust that migrates through shared HVAC systems and settles in places a standard broom-and-mop crew never reaches, inside cabinet boxes, along window tracks, behind appliance panels. In Downtown Bellevue's condo towers, finish-stage cleaning has to deal with manufacturer protective films on windows and countertops, plus the adhesive residue they leave if removed too late or with the wrong solvent. Out toward Somerset and the higher Cougar Mountain-adjacent lots, remodel and addition projects often track in a chalkier, finer soil than you'd find at lower elevations, and it behaves differently under a vacuum than typical job-site dust.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

We start with a walkthrough to flag problem areas: overspray on fixtures, grout haze on new tile, adhesive residue on hardware. Debris and larger construction waste gets staged and hauled first so dust isn't redistributed during detail work. HEPA vacuuming comes next, tackling vents, baseboards, window tracks, and cabinet interiors before any wet cleaning starts, since wiping down dusty surfaces first just smears the problem. Glass, tile, and fixture detailing happens last, along with a final inspection pass that checks sightlines a punch-list reviewer or property manager will actually look at.

## Bellevue HOA Coordination

A lot of our Bellevue work runs through property managers and HOA boards rather than individual homeowners, particularly in the townhome and condo developments built across Crossroads, Factoria, and parts of Lake Hills since the mid-2000s. Many of these associations have their own final-clean checklists tied to move-in approval, and some require documentation before a unit is released to a new owner or tenant. We coordinate directly with the management company or board contact so the cleaning scope matches what they're expecting to sign off on, not just what looks clean to us.

## Local note

Construction sites near Mercer Slough and the lower stretches of Factoria sit on reclaimed wetland soil, and the fine silt that tracks in from site work there doesn't behave like typical upland construction dust. It's chalkier and tends to redistribute under a single vacuum pass rather than lift out, so those jobs usually need a second HEPA pass before wet mopping or the floor looks clean for an hour and then shows haze again once it dries.
