**Storm damage or a break-in left your Vashon property exposed?** Call now for emergency service, and our crew will get plywood and tarps staged for the ferry crossing the moment you call. Falling limbs, wind-driven rain off Puget Sound, and forced entry on seasonal homes all create the same urgent problem: an open structure that lets weather and wildlife in by the hour. Emergency board-up and tarping is about stopping that clock, not cosmetics.

## Why Vashon Properties See Board-Up & Tarping Issues

Vashon Island sits exposed to Puget Sound weather with no bridge connection, which means wind events and fallen trees are a routine cause of broken glass, punctured roofs, and torn siding. Much of the island's housing stock is older farmhouses, post-and-pier cabins, and second homes tucked into heavily wooded lots, so overhanging fir and cedar limbs are a near-constant hazard during fall and winter storms. Vacant vacation properties and weekend homes also see a disproportionate share of break-ins and vandalism simply because no one is there to notice a broken window for days.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Vashon

Because Vashon has limited lumber and hardware supply on the island itself, we load plywood, fasteners, and heavy-mil tarping material before we leave Federal Way so we are not relying on local stock that may be picked over after a regional storm. On site, we measure and cut boards to fit window and door openings rather than using generic panels, which matters on older farmhouse-style homes where frame sizes are rarely standard. Roof tarping gets anchored with batten strips rather than just nails through the tarp, since high wind off the Sound will tear a loosely fastened tarp loose within a day. We photograph every opening before and after for your insurance file, matching the documentation to the IICRC-informed standards our crews are trained on.

## Response Time to Vashon

Reaching Vashon from our Federal Way base means routing through the Point Defiance or Fauntleroy ferry terminals, and we build that crossing time into how we schedule the call rather than pretending it does not exist. We track the Washington State Ferries schedule in real time so a crew isn't left waiting dockside while a roof sits open to the weather. If a storm has backed up ferry traffic island-wide, we communicate that delay to you directly instead of leaving you guessing.

## Local note

A lot of Vashon's exposed board-up calls come from unoccupied weekend and rental cabins near the island's wooded interior, where a downed branch or broken pane can go unnoticed through an entire tide cycle before a neighbor or property manager spots it. We always ask whether a property is owner-occupied or seasonal, because seasonal homes often need a more weather-tight board-up since no one will be checking on it for days or weeks afterward.
