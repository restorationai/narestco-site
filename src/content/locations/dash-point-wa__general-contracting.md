Renovating a home in Dash Point means working around a maritime climate that never really dries out and a building stock that ranges from older waterfront cabins to newer view-lot construction. Whether you're finishing a kitchen remodel, rebuilding a bathroom after a leak, or taking on a full post-damage rebuild, the work has to account for moisture, slope, and the realities of building near Puget Sound.

## Why Dash Point Properties Need a Different Approach

As an unincorporated community in Pierce County, Dash Point sits close enough to the water that salt air, wind-driven rain, and heavy seasonal moisture are part of daily life for every structure here. Homes on sloped or waterfront lots often have crawlspaces or partial foundations that trap humidity, which means framing, subfloor, and cabinetry choices during a remodel need to hold up to conditions a typical inland job wouldn't see. Older homes in the area may also carry lead-based paint or outdated wiring and plumbing that has to be addressed before cosmetic work starts, not after.

## Local note

Waterfront and near-waterfront lots in Dash Point often have drainage patterns that push moisture toward the downhill side of a foundation, which is the side that tends to get skipped during a quick remodel estimate. We check that side specifically before framing starts, because a kitchen or bathroom remodel that looks finished on day one can develop hidden rot within a year if the grading and vapor barrier work underneath isn't addressed first.
