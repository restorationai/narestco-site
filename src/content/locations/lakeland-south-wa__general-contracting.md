## Why Lakeland South Properties Need Renovation Work Done Right the First Time

South King County's housing stock spans several decades, from 1970s and 80s single-story homes to newer developments built after 2000. Older homes in the area often carry original plumbing runs, undersized electrical panels, and single-pane windows, which means a "simple" bathroom or kitchen remodel frequently uncovers a code issue the moment a wall or cabinet comes out. The region's wet winters also mean crawlspaces and lower-level framing can carry moisture that isn't visible until a renovation opens up the space. We account for that before we quote the job, not after we find it.

## Our Renovation and Remodeling Process in Lakeland South

We start every project with a walk-through that separates cosmetic scope from structural scope, because those two things get priced and permitted differently. For kitchen and bathroom remodels, that means checking subfloor condition around tubs and dishwashers before anything gets demoed, since slow leaks under fixtures are common in homes with original plumbing. For post-damage rebuilds, our restoration crews and our construction crews work from the same scope document, so drying and mitigation data carries directly into the rebuild plan instead of getting re-assessed by a second company. Framing, drywall, finish carpentry, and final paint are scheduled in sequence, with inspections built into the timeline rather than squeezed in afterward.

## Permitting Through King County

Much of Lakeland South is unincorporated, which means building permits for remodels and rebuilds typically route through King County rather than a city office. That changes inspection scheduling and turnaround compared to projects inside Kent, Auburn, or Federal Way city limits, and it's a detail that trips up contractors unfamiliar with the area. Our crews pull permits under our General Contractor Certificate of Registration with WA State Labor & Industries and manage the county inspection sequence directly, so homeowners aren't the ones tracking down inspection windows.

## Post-Damage Rebuilds and Insurance Coordination

When a remodel follows a covered loss, fire, water, or wind damage, the construction scope needs to match what the adjuster approved, down to the materials and square footage. We document existing conditions with photos before demo starts and keep the build spec aligned with the insurance estimate throughout, which reduces the back-and-forth that slows down rebuilds. For homeowners who want to upgrade beyond the insurance scope while the walls are already open, we separate that upgrade cost clearly so it doesn't get tangled with the claim.

## Local note

Homes in South King County built before the mid-1990s often have asbestos-containing floor tile or popcorn ceiling texture that wasn't flagged on the original permit. Before we start demo on an older remodel in Lakeland South, we check for these materials as a standard step, not an add-on, because disturbing them without proper handling creates a bigger problem than the one the renovation was meant to solve.
