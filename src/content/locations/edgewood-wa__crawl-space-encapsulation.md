**Crawl space encapsulation in Edgewood** matters more than it does in flatter, drier parts of the South Sound. Pierce County's glacial till soils hold water close to the surface, and homes built on the sloped lots common around Edgewood often sit over crawl spaces that stay damp well into summer. If you've noticed a musty smell rising through your floor vents or insulation sagging and falling away from the subfloor, that's usually ground moisture working its way up, not a one-time leak.

## Why Edgewood Properties See Crawl Space Moisture Issues

Edgewood sits in a part of Pierce County where glacial till and clay-heavy soil layers sit close to the surface. That soil doesn't drain the way sandy or loamy ground does, so when fall and winter rains soak in, water has nowhere to go but sideways, right under a house. Homes on the sloped parcels typical of this area often have crawl spaces set into a hillside grade, which means one side of the foundation can be taking on groundwater pressure while the other stays relatively dry. We also see a lot of older vapor barriers in this area, thin 4-mil plastic sheeting laid loosely over dirt, that was standard practice decades ago but does little against sustained ground moisture or seasonal water table rise.

## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Edgewood

We start with an inspection that checks moisture content in the floor joists and subfloor, looks for signs of wood-destroying organisms, and maps where water is actually entering, whether that's bulk groundwater, condensation off ductwork, or a plumbing drip that's gone unnoticed. From there we remove old insulation and debris, repair or replace damaged framing where needed, and install a sealed vapor barrier rated for direct ground contact, typically 10- to 20-mil reinforced poly depending on soil moisture levels we measure on site. Vents get sealed, seams get taped and mechanically fastened to the foundation wall, and if the crawl space needs active moisture control we install a dehumidifier sized to the square footage rather than a one-size-fits-all unit. For properties with a documented groundwater problem, we'll also price out a sump pump as part of the system rather than treating it as an afterthought.

## Edgewood Insurance & Permit Coordination

Crawl space encapsulation is usually a preventive project rather than an insurance claim, but when it follows a documented water intrusion event, such as a failed sump pump or a burst supply line that soaked the crawl space, we document conditions with photos and moisture readings before starting work so that portion can be submitted to the carrier separately. Pierce County permitting requirements can come into play for structural repairs tied to the encapsulation, like sistering a rotted joist or replacing a support post, and we pull those permits as part of the scope rather than leaving it for the homeowner to sort out afterward.

## Local note

A detail that catches a lot of Edgewood homeowners off guard: sealing a crawl space without first addressing exterior grading or downspout drainage can trap water against the foundation instead of keeping it out. On the sloped lots common in this part of Pierce County, we've found that a vapor barrier alone sometimes just moves the moisture problem rather than solving it, so we check gutter discharge points and grade slope as part of the initial walk-through, not as a separate add-on.
