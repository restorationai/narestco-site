## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Everett

We start with a camera inspection of the supply and return runs so we know whether we're dealing with original 1950s Bayside sheet metal, a newer Silver Lake flex-duct layout, or something in between, since each one calls for a different brush and vacuum head size. From there we seal the registers, connect a negative-air vacuum collection unit to the main trunk line, and agitate each run with a rotating brush so debris pulls toward the collection unit instead of dispersing into the living space. Dryer vent cleaning is handled as a separate pass, since lint compaction in a dryer exhaust run is a fire-risk issue distinct from general HVAC duct buildup and needs its own airflow test afterward. Once the mechanical cleaning is done, we apply an EPA-registered sanitizing treatment to the coil and interior duct surfaces where mold or bacterial growth is a concern, then do a final airflow check at the registers before closing out the job.
