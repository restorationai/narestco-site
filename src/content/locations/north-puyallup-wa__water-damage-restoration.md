North Puyallup sits low relative to the surrounding foothills, and the valley floor holds a high water table for much of the year. When heavy rain or rapid snowmelt off Mt. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in North Puyallup

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable units depending on how the property is set up, then move to moisture mapping with penetrating and non-invasive meters so we know exactly which materials are still wet rather than guessing. We set drying goals based on the specific material (subfloor, drywall, framing) and keep monitoring until those goals are met, which in a crawlspace-heavy North Puyallup home can take longer than it would in a home with open basement access. Dehumidifiers and air movers get placed to work with the building's existing airflow, not against it, and we document moisture readings throughout so the drying record holds up if an adjuster asks for it later.
