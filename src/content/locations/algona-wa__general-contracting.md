National Restoration Construction has handled renovations, remodels, and post-damage rebuilds across South King County since 2004, and we plan Algona projects around what the ground and the house are actually doing, not just what the design calls for.

## Why Algona Properties Need Renovation Planning Built Around the Site

Algona sits low in the Green River Valley, an area with a long regional history of seasonal flooding and slow-draining soil. That matters for anything below grade: a kitchen bump-out with a new footing, a finished basement, or a bathroom remodel that touches plumbing runs under the slab. We check grading and drainage before we price the job, because a renovation that ignores standing water risk at the foundation line ends up costing more in year two than it saved in year one. Add in a housing stock where a meaningful share of homes were built in the postwar decades, often with original wiring and plumbing never upgraded to current code, and a "simple" remodel frequently uncovers knob-and-tube wiring, undersized electrical panels, or cast iron drain lines that need to be addressed before the pretty finishes go in.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Algona

We start with a walkthrough that looks past the scope of work the homeowner asked for, checking the panel, the crawlspace, and the roofline for anything that will stall a permit later. From there we pull the required permits through Algona's building department, sequence demolition and rough-in work so inspections happen in the right order, and keep the homeowner updated at each milestone rather than disappearing until the big reveal. For kitchen and bathroom remodels specifically, we rough in plumbing and electrical to current code even when the existing systems were grandfathered, since a failed rough-in inspection is the single most common reason small remodels run weeks behind schedule. Post-damage rebuilds, where a renovation follows a fire, water loss, or storm event, get an extra layer of documentation so the repair ties cleanly into the insurance claim file.

## Algona Permitting and Lead-Safe Considerations

Because a portion of Algona's housing stock predates 1978, lead-based paint rules apply to any remodel that disturbs painted surfaces in those homes, including window replacement, siding work, and interior demolition. We're a Lead-Safe Certified Firm, which means containment and cleanup on older homes follow EPA RRP protocols instead of generic dust control. That's not optional paperwork; it's what keeps a remodel from exposing a family to lead dust during demo, and it's one more reason to confirm a contractor's certifications before signing a contract, not after the drywall is already open. We also carry a General Contractor Certificate of Registration through the WA State Department of Labor & Industries, which is the credential the city's permit office checks before issuing approvals for structural or electrical work.

## Local note

Homes closer to the valley floor in Algona tend to have crawlspaces with a history of moisture intrusion, even when there's no visible mold or active leak. On remodel jobs that open up flooring or add plumbing fixtures, we check crawlspace vapor barriers and vent screens as a matter of course, because sealing a new bathroom floor over a damp crawlspace just traps the moisture instead of solving it. It's a five-minute check that saves a callback six months later.
