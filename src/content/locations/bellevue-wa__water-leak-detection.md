## Our Water Leak Detection Process in Bellevue

We start with a non-invasive sweep: acoustic listening equipment to pick up the sound of water escaping under pressure, paired with thermal imaging to spot temperature differentials in walls, ceilings, and slabs. For a suspected slab leak, we isolate plumbing zones and run pressure tests to confirm which line is losing pressure before anyone cuts into concrete. In newer Factoria and Crossroads builds with radiant floor heating, we adjust our approach since the detection equipment has to distinguish between heating loop lines and domestic water supply, which read differently on thermal scans. Once a leak is pinpointed, we document the location with photos and moisture readings so you have something concrete to hand to a plumber or your insurance adjuster.
