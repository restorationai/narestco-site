## Why Tacoma Homes Need Specialized Duct Attention

A lot of Tacoma's housing stock predates modern HVAC standards entirely. Homes in North Tacoma and the Proctor District were often built with additions and furnace replacements layered on over decades, which means duct transitions, elbow joints, and old sheet-metal seams accumulate dust, pet dander, and in some cases rodent activity in crawlspaces that never had proper sealing. Add in the salt-laden air that rolls in off Ruston Way and Point Defiance Park, and metal ductwork in homes closer to the waterfront corrodes faster than it would further inland, which creates rough interior surfaces where debris catches and builds up instead of moving through the system. ## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Tacoma

We start with a camera inspection of the supply and return lines so you can actually see what's accumulated before any work begins, which matters most in older Hilltop and Old Town homes where previous duct work may be undocumented. From there we use negative-pressure HEPA vacuum equipment to pull contaminants out of the system rather than just agitating them, paired with rotary brushes sized to the duct diameter for the stubborn buildup that's typical in galvanized runs from that era. Tacoma's municipal rules on HEPA filtration for restoration work that generates airborne particulate apply directly to this kind of job, so containment at the register openings isn't optional, it's how the work gets done correctly whether or not a given house happens to need it for allergy reasons. We finish with a sanitization pass on request, which matters more in homes that have had any history of moisture intrusion, since Tacoma's humidity gives mold spores in ductwork a head start that drier climates don't.

## Dryer Vent Cleaning for Tacoma's Older Floor Plans

Dryer vent cleaning gets overlooked next to the main duct system, but it's often the more urgent issue. Many homes around Wright Park and North Tacoma have dryer vent runs that were extended during remodels to reach an exterior wall on the opposite side of the house, and those longer runs with extra bends are exactly where lint compacts and airflow drops, which is a fire risk the manufacturer's vent length guidelines exist to prevent. We clear the full run to the exterior termination, not just the accessible section behind the dryer, and check that the exterior flap is sealing properly against the marine air that corrodes louvered vent covers faster here than in drier parts of the state.
