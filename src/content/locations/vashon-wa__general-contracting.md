Renovating a home on Vashon Island means working around a ferry schedule as much as a floor plan. Whether you're updating a 1970s cabin near the center of the island or finishing a post-damage rebuild after a burst pipe or storm event, the project timeline has to account for the fact that every truckload of cabinets, tile, or lumber crosses Puget Sound to get here. National Restoration Construction plans Vashon remodels around that reality instead of fighting it.

## Why Vashon Properties See Renovations & GC Challenges

Vashon's housing stock runs the range from early-1900s farmhouses to newer builds tucked into wooded lots, and a large share of island homes still rely on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal water and sewer. That changes how a kitchen or bathroom remodel gets scoped: moving plumbing fixtures isn't just a matter of running new supply lines, it can mean coordinating with a septic designer before a permit gets issued. The island's damp, marine climate also puts extra stress on exterior-facing additions and any framing that sits close to grade, so moisture management gets built into the plan from the start rather than treated as an afterthought.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process on Vashon Island

We start with an on-site walkthrough to document existing conditions, well and septic locations, and any structural quirks typical of older island construction. Material lists get finalized early so deliveries can be batched for a single ferry crossing rather than trickling in over several trips, which keeps labor crews from standing idle waiting on a load of drywall or fixtures. Once King County permitting is in hand, we sequence demo, rough-in, and finish work in stages that match what can realistically be staged and stored on a semi-rural lot. For post-damage rebuilds, we document the original loss thoroughly before any demo begins, which keeps the insurance side of the project moving in parallel with construction instead of stalling it.

## Vashon Permitting & Insurance Coordination

Most remodeling and rebuild work on the island falls under King County's permitting authority since Vashon is unincorporated, and that process tends to move on a different timeline than permits inside Seattle or Tacoma city limits. We handle the permit submission and inspection scheduling directly so homeowners aren't shuttling paperwork back and forth across the ferry themselves. When a remodel is tied to a covered loss, fire, water, or storm damage, we document the pre-existing condition and the scope of repair clearly enough for most major carriers to process the claim without repeated site visits, which matters more here than in places where an adjuster can just drive back over for a second look.

## Local note

Material deliveries on Vashon run on Washington State Ferries' schedule, not a supplier's dispatch board. We've learned to build a day or two of buffer into any order that needs to arrive via the Fauntleroy or Tahlequah crossings, because a missed sailing on a Friday afternoon can mean cabinets or tile sit on the mainland until Monday. Scheduling trade crews around that same rhythm, rather than assuming mainland lead times apply, is one of the quieter ways a remodel on the island stays on budget instead of sliding past it.
