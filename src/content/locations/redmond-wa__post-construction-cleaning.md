## Equipment & Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

HEPA-filtered vacuums are non-negotiable on anything involving drywall sanding or demo dust, since standard shop vacs just recirculate the finest particles back into the air. For adhesive and paint overspray on glass and tile, we use mechanical scraping before chemical treatment to avoid clouding new finishes. Microfiber systems replace cotton rags for nearly everything in the final pass, because they lift dust rather than push it around, which matters most on dark cabinetry and stainless appliances where streaking shows immediately.
