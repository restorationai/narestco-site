## Why Des Moines Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

Sewage intrusion is classified as Category 3 water under the IICRC S500 standard, meaning everything it touched, carpet pad, drywall, insulation, is treated as contaminated and removed rather than dried in place. Crews arrive in full PPE, contain the affected area to stop cross-contamination into HVAC returns or adjoining rooms, and pump out standing waste before extracting residual moisture from subfloor and framing. Porous materials that absorbed sewage, baseboards, carpet, sheetrock up to the flood line, are bagged and disposed of according to state waste-handling guidelines rather than cleaned, since pathogens embed in the fiber structure and can't be reliably removed. Once the structure is stripped back to sanitizable surfaces, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments, then set negative air pressure and dehumidification to bring the space back to a normal moisture reading before any reconstruction begins.
