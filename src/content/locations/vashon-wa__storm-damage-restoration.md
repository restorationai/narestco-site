Vashon's exposed bluffs and dense fir and cedar cover mean a single windstorm can bring down limbs through a roofline, drive rain sideways under siding, and knock out island power for longer than the mainland sees, all in the same afternoon. ## Why Vashon Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Vashon is an island, and that single fact shapes almost every storm loss we see here. Many homes sit on wooded lots with mature evergreens close to the roofline, so windstorms bring branch and whole-tree strikes more often than they bring simple wind-driven rain. A lot of the housing stock is rural and older, built before current wind-load and drainage codes, with crawlspaces and septic systems that are more vulnerable to saturated ground than a typical Federal Way or Tacoma lot. Because the island runs on a single utility feed from Puget Sound Energy, outages after a storm tend to last longer than on the mainland grid, which matters when a sump pump stops running or a dehumidifier needs to run continuously to keep a wet structure from turning into a mold problem.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Vashon

We start with an exterior assessment to stop the active damage: tarping a compromised roof section, boarding broken windows, and clearing fallen limbs that are blocking safe access to the structure. Once the property is secured, we move inside to extract standing water and document everything for your insurance carrier, including moisture readings on framing and subfloor that aren't visible to the eye. Drying on Vashon often takes longer than a comparable mainland job because island humidity stays higher near the water, and older homes with original wood subfloors or plaster release moisture more slowly than modern drywall and OSB. We monitor with moisture meters and adjust drying equipment placement daily rather than assuming a standard timeline will hold.

## Response Time to Vashon

Reaching Vashon means routing through the Fauntleroy-Vashon ferry or the Point Defiance-Tahlequah run from the south, and we factor that into how we schedule crews so a storm call doesn't sit waiting on a sailing. Our crews based out of Federal Way plan routes around ferry timing and island road conditions, including downed trees that regularly close sections of the main roads after a windstorm. If your property is harder to reach, whether that's a gravel lane off a main route or a lot where a fallen tree is blocking the driveway itself, tell us when you call so we can plan the right equipment and, if needed, coordinate tree removal before our crew arrives.

## Vashon Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most of Vashon is unincorporated King County, so there's no single municipal permitting office to coordinate with the way there might be in an incorporated city, but King County still issues permits for structural repair work tied to storm damage. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings formatted for your carrier, and we bill most major insurance companies directly for the restoration portion of the work. If your property is part of one of the island's smaller homeowner associations, we'll work with whatever approval process they require for exterior repairs, though most Vashon storm claims we see move through the standard carrier process without additional HOA steps.

## Local note

A lot of Vashon homes still rely on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal water and sewer, and storm flooding around a wellhead or septic field is a different problem than it is on a municipal system: it can mean a water quality test is warranted before anyone drinks from the tap again, and it changes how we handle grading and drainage repairs around the structure so runoff doesn't pool back toward the well in the next storm.
